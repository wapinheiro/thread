# Thred: Governance

To ensure Thred serves every stakeholder fairly as it grows to include
more component programs, governance is shared rather than owned by a
single office.

## The Steering Committee

**Mandate:**
- Approve new Tracks
- Validate Market GPA weightings and scoring changes
- Review early-warning / at-risk cohort data
- Approve new component programs joining the ecosystem (see
  [Partner Registry](PARTNER_REGISTRY.md))

**Composition:**
- **Chair:** Department Director or Associate Chair
- **Faculty Representative** — curriculum alignment
- **Academic Advisor** — graduation requirement alignment
- **Career Advisor** — market alignment
- **External Relations Representative** — industry alignment
- *(as the ecosystem grows)* representation from active component
  programs, on a rotating or as-needed basis

**Cadence:** Quarterly review of ecosystem-wide readiness reporting.

## Decision Boundaries

- **Core program changes** (journey structure, scoring formula, data
  model) require Steering Committee approval.
- **Component program changes** (how a specific partner program runs
  internally) are owned by that program, as long as its integration
  points with Thred continue to match its registered
  [Partner Template](../partners/TEMPLATE.md).
- **New component programs** are proposed via the Partner Template and
  approved by the Steering Committee before being onboarded into the
  Resource Registry.
