# Thred: Partner Registry

The Partner Registry is what makes Thred an umbrella rather than a
single, monolithic program. Any BYU CS initiative aimed at student market
preparedness — a milestone/sticker program, an alumni group running
events, a club, a future idea not yet imagined — can plug in as a
**Component Program**.

## Why Register as a Component Program?

- **Visibility:** the program surfaces directly inside a student's plan
  (Journey Stage 5 — "Get Connected") at the moment it's relevant to
  their actual gaps, instead of relying on a flyer, a Slack channel, or
  word of mouth.
- **Shared record:** engagement with the program can (optionally)
  contribute to a student's Market GPA, and always contributes to their
  continuous record of preparation.
- **Shared audience:** the program gains access to Thred's broader
  reporting — e.g., how many students engaged, what gaps it's closing —
  without having to build that infrastructure itself.

## What Registering Does *Not* Require

- A component program does not need to adopt Thred's visual identity,
  tone, or internal processes.
- A component program does not need to feed Market GPA at all — some
  programs (e.g., a networking-focused alumni group) may be more useful
  tracked separately, as their own kind of evidence, rather than folded
  into a single number. This is a deliberate choice made per-program; see
  [Partner Template](../partners/TEMPLATE.md).
- A component program does not need Steering Committee involvement for
  its own internal operations — only for its integration points with
  Thred.

## Two Integration Points

Every component program integrates with Thred at exactly two places in
the [Journey](../core/JOURNEY.md):

1. **Stage 5 — Get Connected:** the program registers itself as a
   **Resource** mapped to one or more skills/Gaps, so Thred can surface
   it to relevant students.
2. **Stage 7 — Do the Work:** the program defines what counts as an
   **Artifact** — the evidence a student produces by participating (a
   sticker earned, an event attended, a project completed) — and how
   that evidence is verified.

## Onboarding Process

1. Program owner completes the [Partner Template](../partners/TEMPLATE.md).
2. Steering Committee reviews and approves.
3. Program is added to the Resource Registry with its defined resources
   and Artifact types.
4. Program appears in relevant students' plans going forward.

See existing examples: [Milestone Stickers](../partners/milestone-stickers.md),
[Alumni Tech Society](../partners/alumni-tech-society.md).
