# Thred: Stakeholders

A summary of who participates in the Thred ecosystem, what they gain,
and what they contribute. See [Strategic Proposal](STRATEGIC_PROPOSAL.md)
for full reasoning.

| Stakeholder | Benefit | Participation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Students** | A single, continuous, verifiable record of their preparation across every program they engage with. | Complete their Journey; submit Artifacts. |
| **Academic Advisors** | Offloads repetitive questions; early-warning dashboard for at-risk students. | Use readiness data to intervene proactively. |
| **Career Advisors** | Scales impact beyond 1-on-1 capacity. | Review plans; validate Artifacts; provide nuance software can't. |
| **Faculty** | Visibility into student intent and skill gaps across the student body; can act as signal validators. | Optionally link coursework outcomes (e.g., capstone grades) to Market GPA. |
| **Alumni / External Relations** | A structured way to reach current students; concrete data to pitch corporate partners. | Register as a component program; keep target skills current based on market demand. |
| **Department Leadership** | Real-time, defensible reporting on student readiness across the department. | Sponsor governance; review quarterly ecosystem reports. |
| **Component Program Owners** (clubs, milestone programs, etc.) | Built-in visibility to the right students at the right time; access to shared reporting. | Register via the [Partner Template](../partners/TEMPLATE.md); maintain their own Artifact/resource definitions. |
